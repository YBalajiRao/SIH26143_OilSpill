2. NTRO PROBLEM STATEMENT & CORE MANDATE
========================================================================================================================
Official Description:
"Marine oil spills inflict great damage on marine ecosystems and several times remain un-attributable to the 
vessel causing such spills. Leveraging satellite imagery along with AIS data will enable detection of oil spills 
and vessel responsible for the same.

The core challenge attempts to facilitate detection of oil spills and also in identifying the polluting vessel using 
remote sensing satellite data, such as SAR and EO imagery and AIS data. Participants are to design an intelligent 
automated pipeline to do the following: 
  (a) Detect and characterise the oil spill and calculating geometric properties and age if feasible. 
  (b) Using oceanographic and meteorological data, it is envisaged to trace the slick towards the origin point and time, 
      predict the future flow of the slick, and 
  (c) analyse and attribute the spill to a vessel using historic AIS data to reconstruct vessel traffic around the origin 
      window in space and time. The irrelevant traffic is to be filtered out and potential suspect vessels are to be 
      scored considering various aspects such as proximity, trajectory, behavioural anomalies etc.

Expected Solution: An automated detection and hindcasting machine learning model that identified oil slicks from 
satellite imagery, mapping their drift paths backward and forward. It also ranks potential culprit vessel based on 
spatio-temporal correlation with AIS data. A suitable visual interface is also to be developed."

3. RESEARCH OBJECTIVES & 3 PILLARS
========================================================================================================================
PILLAR 1: ROBUST MARINE PERCEPTION
  - Objective 1: Detect oil spills robustly from Sentinel-1 SAR imagery under multiplicative speckle noise, low 
                 backscatter contrast, and look-alike interferences.
  - Objective 2: Compute geometric properties (Slick Area, Perimeter, Centroid, Orientation Angle, Elongation Ratio).
  - Objective 3: Estimate slick age post-release based on empirical Fay spreading laws and radar backscatter contrast.
  - Objective 4: Quantify pixel-level epistemic uncertainty (sigma) via Monte Carlo Dropout (T=10 stochastic passes).

PILLAR 2: UNCERTAINTY-AWARE DRIFT HINDCASTING & FORECASTING
  - Objective 5: Ingest real NetCDF4 spatiotemporal metocean forcing fields (ERA5 10m wind + CMEMS surface currents).
  - Objective 6: Back-propagate 1,000 Lagrangian particles over -24h to reconstruct the release origin probability peak.
  - Objective 7: Propagate 1,000 Lagrangian particles +24h forward in time to forecast coastal impact zones.

PILLAR 3: SPATIOTEMPORAL AIS ATTRIBUTION & DECISION SUPPORT
  - Objective 8: Decompress and parse multi-gigabyte NOAA MarineCadastre AIS vessel traffic matching official schemas.
  - Objective 9: Filter out distant/irrelevant maritime traffic using spatiotemporal bounding cones.
  - Objective 10: Rank suspect vessels using NTRO Multi-Factor Scoring: Spatial Proximity (50%), Kinematic Speed (25%), 
                  Course-Over-Ground Trajectory Alignment (25%), and AIS Transmission Gap Penalties (-20%).
  - Objective 11: Export legally defensible NTRO Evidentiary Dossiers (JSON) and host an Interactive Web Dashboard (HTML).


========================================================================================================================
4. NOVELTY & TECHNICAL INNOVATIONS
========================================================================================================================
1. Atrous Degradation-Aware Feature Modulation (DAFM): Dynamically extracts multi-scale speckle profiles using 
   dilated convolutions (dilation = 1, 2, 4) and modulates skip connections via Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM).
2. ASPP Receptive Field Expansion: Integrates Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling at the encoder bottleneck to preserve 
   global sea-state context under severe satellite noise.
3. Bayesian Kinematic Trajectory Alignment (Delta Theta): Scores vessels by computing the angular difference between 
   the slick's major orientation axis and the vessel's Course Over Ground (COG).
4. Dual Lagrangian Particle Engine: Simulates both backward hindcast origin peaks and forward impact probability maps.
5. Metocean Sensitivity Monte Carlo Testing: Perturbs wind leeway (2.5% to 4.5%) across simulation trials to prove 
   attributable vessel rank stability under weather uncertainty.
