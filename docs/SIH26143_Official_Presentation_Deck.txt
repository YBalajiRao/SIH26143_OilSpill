SLIDE 2: PROBLEM STATEMENT & NTRO REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background: Marine oil spills inflict devastating damage on coastal ecosystems, yet often remain un-attributed to polluting vessels.
- Core NTRO Challenge: Build an automated software pipeline to:
  1. Detect & characterize oil spills from Sentinel-1 SAR imagery.
  2. Compute geometric properties (Area, Elongation, Orientation) & Slick Age.
  3. Perform Backward Lagrangian Drift Hindcasting (-24h Origin) & Forward Flow Forecasting (+24h Impact).
  4. Reconstruct AIS traffic around release origin window, filter irrelevant traffic, and rank suspect vessels.
  5. Provide an interactive visual decision support interface.

SLIDE 3: PHYSIO-GRAPHSPILL NOVEL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Perception: ResNet-34 Encoder + Atrous Degradation-Aware Feature Modulation (DAFM) + ASPP Bottleneck.
- Uncertainty: Monte Carlo Dropout (T=10) spatial epistemic uncertainty maps (sigma) for boundary confidence.
- Metocean Physics Engine: ERA5 10m Wind + CMEMS Surface Currents (3.5% wind leeway).
- Attribution Engine: NTRO Multi-Factor Scoring (70% Proximity, 30% Kinematics, -20% AIS Gap Penalty).
- Visual Evidence: Interactive Web Dashboard & 6-Panel Intelligence Map.

SLIDE 5: DEGRADATION STRESS TEST & ROBUSTNESS (EXPERIMENT E6)

- U-Net collapses under severe noise (drops to 64.02% mIoU / 40.73% Dice).
