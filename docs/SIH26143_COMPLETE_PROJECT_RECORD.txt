2. NTRO OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT
===============================================
Title      : Leveraging satellite imagery to determine Oil spills at sea along with AIS data 
             correlations to identify vessel responsible for the spill.

Background : Marine oil spills inflict great damage on marine ecosystems and several times 
             remain un-attributable to the vessel causing such spills. Leveraging satellite 
             imagery along with AIS data will enable detection of oil spills and vessel 
             responsible for the same.

Description: The core challenge attempts to facilitate detection of oil spills and also 
             identifying the polluting vessel using remote sensing satellite data (SAR & EO 
             imagery) and AIS data. Participants are to design an intelligent automated pipeline 
             to:
             (a) Detect and characterise the oil spill and calculate geometric properties and age.
             (b) Trace the slick towards the origin point and time using oceanographic and 
                 meteorological data, and predict the future flow of the slick.
             (c) Attribute the spill to a vessel using historic AIS data to reconstruct vessel 
                 traffic around the origin window in space and time. Filter out irrelevant 
                 traffic. Score suspect vessels based on proximity, trajectory, and behavioural 
                 anomalies.

Expected Solution: An automated detection and hindcasting machine learning model that identifies 
                   oil slicks from satellite imagery, maps drift paths backward and forward, and 
                   ranks potential culprit vessels based on spatio-temporal correlation with AIS 
                   data. A suitable visual interface is also to be developed.


===============================================
3. RESEARCH OBJECTIVES
===============================================
1.  Detect marine oil spills robustly from Sentinel-1 SAR imagery under speckle, weak contrast, 
    and look-alike interferences.
2.  Characterise slick geometry: Area, Perimeter, Centroid, Orientation, and Elongation.
3.  Estimate slick age based on empirical Fay spreading law and radar backscatter contrast.
4.  Estimate probable release location and time as a probability distribution.
5.  Perform backward Lagrangian drift hindcasting using currents, wind, and stochastic diffusion.
6.  Forecast future slick movement using forward Lagrangian drift.
7.  Reconstruct historical AIS traffic around the origin window using real MarineCadastre data.
8.  Filter irrelevant vessels via spatial and temporal reachability filters.
9.  Rank candidate vessels using trajectory proximity, kinematic match, and AIS gap anomalies.
10. Provide an interactive web dashboard for defence analysts.


===============================================
4. NOVELTY & CONTRIBUTIONS
===============================================
1. Constrained Inverse-Transport Attribution: Uses backward Lagrangian particle drift to 
   probabilistically trace release origin, not nearest-vessel heuristics.
2. Uncertainty Propagation: MC Dropout (T=10) pixel-level epistemic uncertainty feeds into 
   downstream drift particle weighting.
3. Heterogeneous Spatio-Temporal Ranking: Multi-factor NTRO scoring across proximity, 
   kinematics, and behavioural AIS anomalies.
4. Physics-Guided Counterfactual Simulation: Uses synthetic discharge event training when 
   real culprit labels are scarce.
5. Degradation-Aware SAR Perception: Atrous DAFM (Dilated Feature Modulation) + ASPP 
   bottleneck robust to satellite noise.
6. Event-level Geographic Generalization Evaluation across Gulf of Mexico + Zenodo benchmarks.
